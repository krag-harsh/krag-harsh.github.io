I am an ML researcher working on generative models, multimodal learning, and embodied AI. I am currently a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at [ENSIMAG and UFR IM²AG](https://ensimag.grenoble-inp.fr/) (Grenoble, France), conducting my thesis research as an ML Research Intern at [INRIA Grenoble's RobotLearn team](https://team.inria.fr/robotlearn/), advised by [Dr. Xavier Alameda-Pineda](https://xavirema.eu/). My current work focuses on flow-matching models for multimodal action generation — developing generative architectures that condition robot behavior on visual, auditory, and linguistic context.

Before my Master's, I worked as a Software/Firmware Developer at IBM (2023–2025), building redundant BMC architectures and firmware tooling for POWER server systems. Prior to that, I conducted undergraduate research at the [VISION Lab, IIIT Delhi](https://vision-iiitd.github.io/) under [Dr. Saket Anand](https://faculty.iiitd.ac.in/~anands/), where I worked on multi-spectral satellite imagery analysis for precision agriculture — resulting in a [dataset paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2024/html/Sani_SICKLE_A_Multi-Sensor_Satellite_Imagery_Dataset_Annotated_With_Multiple_Key_WACV_2024_paper.html) accepted for oral presentation at WACV 2024.

I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science (BioScience specialization) at [IIIT Delhi](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/) in 2023 with a GPA of 8.83.
